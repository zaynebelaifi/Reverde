Reading the diagram

Associations

  • A forest zone has weather records and fire incidents.
  • ForestSpecies joins forest zones and species. Observations refer to both.
  • Fire incidents can have damage assessments and lead to recovery projects.
  • Volunteer registrations associate volunteers with activities. Equipment allocations can support recovery projects.

Business rules

  • Incidents start as REPORTED, may become VERIFIED, and are eventually CLOSED.
  • Weather data drives risk assessments using configurable thresholds.
  • Recovery projects record planting objectives and progress.
  • The multiplicities are the proposed Session 4 model.

Enumerations

  • IncidentStatus: REPORTED, VERIFIED, CLOSED.
  • RiskLevel: LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, VERY_HIGH.
