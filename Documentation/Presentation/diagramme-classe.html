Overall UML class diagram

This conceptual model connects the main entities in ReVerde, from forest biodiversity and weather records to fire incidents, recovery projects, and field operations.

Reading the diagram

  • Forest biodiversity. ForestSpecies connects forest zones to plant and wildlife species. BiodiversityObservation records observations of a species in a zone.
  • Risk and incidents. Weather records support risk assessments. A fire incident references its forest zone and can have damage assessments.
  • Recovery. A recovery project can reference a fire incident and contain planting records. Activities and equipment allocations can support a project.
  • Multiplicity. The labels show the proposed rules for Session 4. For example, 0..* means zero or more associated records.
