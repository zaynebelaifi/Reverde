UML class diagram by service

This view groups the classes by service ownership and shows sample fields. The dashed arrows show proposed references between services.

Reading the diagram

  • Forest and risk. The forest service owns ForestZone. The risk service can store forestZoneId on weather records.
  • Incidents and recovery. The incident service owns FireIncident and DamageAssessment. The recovery service can use incidentId to link a project to a reported fire.
  • Volunteers and equipment. Volunteer activities and resource allocations can reference a recovery project by ID.
  • Service boundaries. Cross-service IDs refer to records managed by another service. Each service owns and maintains its local database relationships.
